1. **Ronald L. Graham, Donald E. Knuth, Oren Patashnik, Concrete Mathematics (2nd Edition).**
   - Comprehensive coverage of factorials, combinatorics, and their applications.

2. **Peter J. Cameron, Combinatorics: Topics, Techniques, Algorithms.**
   - Explains permutations, combinations, and factorial growth in problem solving.

3. **Wikipedia: Factorial**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorial
   - Definitions, properties, and applications of factorials.

4. **Paul Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving.**
   - Problem-solving strategies and factorial-based puzzles.

5. **NPTEL: Discrete Mathematics and Combinatorics**
   - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111/106/111106086/
   - Video lectures and notes on combinatorics and factorials.
